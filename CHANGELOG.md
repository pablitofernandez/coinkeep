# Changelog

What changes in Coinkeep from one version to the next, written for players. The game shows this same list on the title screen (**📜 What's new**). The Spanish version is in [CHANGELOG.es.md](CHANGELOG.es.md).

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-11

### New
- **Cards:** after every 2nd wave the game pauses and offers you 3 cards; keep one. There are 28, from sharper arrows and faster boots to boiling oil on the walls, a free peon or extra damage against bosses.
  - **Common**, **rare** (blue) and **epic** (purple). After a boss wave, at least one of the three is rare or better.
  - Two epic cards come with a price: **Blood pact** (more damage, less castle health) and **Gold fever** (more coins, more enemies).
  - Don't like the hand? **🎲 New hand** swaps the three cards for coins you carry; each extra one in the same round costs more.
  - Your cards are shown under the wave counter. On a keyboard, choose with 1, 2, 3 and press R for a new hand.

### Balance
- From wave 30 on, enemies get tougher faster, so very long games come to an end.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-11-11

### Balance
- Wiping out a whole wave now patches up the castle: it recovers 12% of its maximum health.
- Bosses are a bit less tough: 6 times the health of a warrior instead of 8.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-11

### New
- The game is now in **English and Spanish**. It follows your system language, and you can switch with the **EN | ES** buttons on the title screen.
- **📜 What's new** on the title screen: this list of changes, with a dot on the button when there is something you haven't seen yet.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-11-11

### Fixes
- Fairer leaderboard: records with impossible waves or times are rejected, and names can no longer contain invisible or special characters.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-10

### New
- **Skeleton army:** enemies are now skeleton warriors, and every few waves a giant skeleton boss shows up.
- **New enemies:**
  - **Minion** (from wave 3): half the health, but much faster.
  - **Bat** (from wave 6): flies over trees and rocks, and cannons can't hit it.
  - **Mage** (from wave 8): heals nearby enemies every few seconds.
- **Difficulty levels:** Normal, Hard and Nightmare, chosen on the title screen.
- **Global leaderboard:** when you lose, enter your name to see your rank and the top 10 for that difficulty.
- Each enemy type has its own death sound.

### Balance
- Enemy health grows faster with each wave, and waves bring more enemies, depending on the difficulty.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-10

First public version, playable in the browser on desktop and mobile.

### New
- Defend the castle from waves of enemies: kill them, collect their coins and carry them to the plots to build archer towers and cannons, upgrade them, strengthen your hero, forge better weapons for everyone, repair the castle and hire wolves and peons.
- Title screen with the Coinkeep logo.
- Fantasy music and sound effects.

### Fixes
- Smoother on mobile, especially on iPhone, and no accidental browser zoom.
- Enemies no longer get stuck on fallen logs.
